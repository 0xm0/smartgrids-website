The placement brain

iDOE Placement
Engine

iDOE — the Intelligent Distributed Orchestration Engine — decides where every workload runs. It operates on nine resource dimensions: compute capacity, memory, storage, network bandwidth, GPU availability, sovereignty zone, security clearance, latency budget, and energy cost.

SmartGrids AI — iDOE Policy Engine  ·  9 Dimensions

It is a two-stage system: first eliminate every resource that fails hard constraints — sovereignty zone, security clearance, capability minimums. Then rank the survivors by SLO alignment — latency, cost, energy efficiency. The golden rule: policy determines eligibility, optimisation determines preference.

iDOE is the foundation of a longer programme. As the mesh grows, placement becomes richer — more nodes, more capability diversity, more policy dimensions. The engine scales with the fabric.

The total cost of generating one unit of energy over a system's lifetime. Solar LCOE in Australia has fallen below grid parity, making distributed solar-powered compute economically superior to grid-connected hyperscale facilities.
